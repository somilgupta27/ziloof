Our Story

Every bottle has a beginning.
Ours begins with a question.

Why should something as essential as mustard oil leave you wondering what’s really inside?

For generations, mustard oil has been at the heart of Indian kitchens. Somewhere along the way, purity became harder to find. We went back to what mattered most.

Carefully selected mustard seeds. A single cold press. No unnecessary processing. Just the bold aroma, authentic flavor and natural character mustard oil is meant to have.

“If we wouldn’t serve it to our own family, we won’t bottle it for yours.”
